DELETE YOUR UNDRIFTLY ACCOUNT AND DATA
Last updated July 24, 2026
This page explains how to request deletion of your Undriftly account and its associated data, and what is deleted or kept afterwards. Undriftly is developed by Sakthikailash SV. If you use Undriftly without signing in, no data is ever sent to our servers — uninstalling the app removes your on-device data, and there is no account to delete.
HOW TO REQUEST DELETION
Option A — In the app (fastest)
  1. Open the Undriftly app.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm.
Your account access is disabled immediately, and your backend data is permanently purged within 24 hours. No further action is needed.
Option B — By email (if you cannot access the app)
Email moodsavvy.app@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject “Delete my Undriftly account.” We may need to verify your identity before completing the request. Verified requests are completed within 30 days (usually much sooner).
Note: Deleting your Undriftly account does not cancel Google Play billing. If you subscribed to Unlimited, cancel the subscription separately in Google Play → Subscriptions to stop future charges.
WHAT DATA IS DELETED
When your account is deleted, we permanently remove the following from our backend:
  • Your account and sign-in identity — email address, authentication credentials, and account identifier;
  • Your profile, including any display name you chose;
  • Focus-session records synced to your account — opaque session keys, active-time and start/end timestamps, and server-side usage-metering records;
  • Any server-side per-session allowed-app selections;
  • Your subscription / entitlement records;
  • Optional social data — your public display name, friend connections, body-double room membership and presence, one-time invite records, and any abuse reports you submitted;
  • Any pending data-export jobs.
Your detailed focus content — task titles, descriptions, notes, check-in answers, scores, drift reasons, and your Intent Guard app selections — is never uploaded to our servers. It stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall Undriftly or clear its app data.
WHAT DATA IS KEPT, AND FOR HOW LONG
  • Account, profile, focus-session, usage, entitlement, and social records — deleted within 24 hours of an in-app request (or within 30 days of a verified email request).
  • Billing / purchase records we are legally required to retain (for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention) — kept in a detached, de-identified form, unlinked from your identity, for the period required by law.
  • Google Play transaction records — held by Google Play under Google’s own policies, outside our control.
  • Security / operational logs (such as IP address and request timestamps) — retained by our infrastructure providers for a short period for security and fraud prevention, then deleted.
DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE
Data kept solely on your device — including the 30-day deleted-session archive available to Unlimited users — is not held on our servers. Uninstalling Undriftly or clearing its app data removes that local data from your device.
HOW CAN YOU CONTACT US?
If you have questions about deleting your account or data, contact Sakthikailash SV at moodsavvy.app@gmail.com. You can also review our Privacy Policy.
